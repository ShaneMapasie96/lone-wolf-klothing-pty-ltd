How to Order

  1. Choose your pieces. Select your design, garment color, logo color and size, then add your pieces to the cart.
  2. Send your WhatsApp enquiry. Open your cart, enter your contact and delivery details, then select “Order on WhatsApp” and send the message.
  3. Receive confirmation and payment details. We confirm availability, delivery costs and your final order total on WhatsApp.
  4. Pay your 50% deposit. Use the payment details provided and send proof of payment. We begin processing once your deposit is received and verified.
  5. Settle the remaining 50%. Pay the balance before your order is dispatched or collected.
