Sizing & Fit Guide

Choose the fit that feels right for you. Most products are true to size; hoodies often need one size up. For the styles below, your usual size gives a closer fit, while one size up gives more room.

Crewnecks, hoodies, sweatpants & shorts

Choose your usual size if you prefer a closer, more fitted feel. Choose one size up if you prefer a looser fit with less tightness. For hoodies, we generally recommend sizing up for a comfortable fit.

For example, if you usually wear Medium in a hoodie or crewneck, you may prefer Large for extra room. If you usually wear Small in sweatpants or trackpants, Medium may feel less tight. These are fit examples, not a requirement to size up.

Body warmers & jackets

The same choice applies: your usual size for a closer fit, or one size up for a looser fit. Think about the clothing you plan to wear underneath when deciding how much room you want.

Other products

Other products are true to size: start with your usual size. For headwear, follow the size options shown on the product. OSFA means one size fits all; adjustable caps can be adjusted to suit your fit.

For tracksuit sets, consider how you like both the top and trackpants to fit. If you need different sizes for each piece, ask us before ordering to confirm the available options.

Compare with something you already wear

Lay a similar garment that fits you well flat, without stretching it. For tops and outerwear, measure across the chest from underarm to underarm and from the shoulder to the hem. For bottoms, measure across the waistband and from the crotch seam to the hem along the inside leg.

These are garment measurements, not body measurements. Keep the tape straight and note your measurements in centimetres. Tell us which product and size you are considering and whether you prefer a closer or looser fit, so we can help you check before ordering.

Need help choosing?

For product-specific measurements and sizing help, contact us or ask us on WhatsApp before placing your order.
