THE COURIER GUY LOCKER DELIVERY

Collect your order from a convenient The Courier Guy locker in South Africa.

Estimated delivery is 1–4 business days after dispatch.

When your parcel arrives, use your collection PIN or QR code to open the locker. Collect within the time shown in your delivery notification.

Many lockers are accessible 24/7. Lockers inside shopping centres follow the centre's opening hours.

Find your nearest The Courier Guy locker

FREE DELIVERY ON ORDERS OVER R750

When your order total exceeds R750, Lone Wolf Klothing pays the delivery fee for delivery to your chosen The Courier Guy locker.

DELIVERY PRICING

For orders of R750 or less, you pay The Courier Guy's quoted locker delivery rate, with no markup from Lone Wolf Klothing.

The price depends on your parcel size, weight and destination. Check The Courier Guy's current delivery quote or contact us for the delivery cost for your order.
